Privacy Policy

Physical Activity Gate  ·  Last updated: November 10, 2026

Physical Activity Gate is designed with privacy as a foundation. Your exercise data, activity history, and device usage statistics never leave your phone. No account is required. No data is sent to any server.

1. Information We Collect

Physical Activity Gate does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on external servers. All data generated by the app lives exclusively on your device in a local SQLite database:

  • Exercise sessions — type of exercise, repetition count, completion status, and timestamps.
  • Activity rules — which apps you choose to protect and the exercise requirements you configure.
  • Daily statistics — aggregate counts of exercises completed, unlocks, and streaks.
  • Authorization state — which protected apps are currently unlocked and when that access expires.
  • App settings — your preferences such as authorization duration, bypass mode, and PIN hash (stored as a one-way hash, never plain text).

2. Camera Usage

The camera is used only when you start a camera-verified exercise (squats, push-ups, jumping jacks, sit-ups, or plank). Camera frames are processed entirely on your device using on-device ML Kit pose detection. Camera frames are never recorded, stored, or transmitted. The camera is released immediately when you complete the activity, cancel, or leave the exercise screen. You can disable camera verification in Settings at any time; manual self-confirmation remains available without camera access.

3. Step Counter Sensor

When you select Steps as your exercise type, the app reads your device's built-in step sensor. This data is used only to count steps during an active session and is never stored beyond the session's completion record.

4. Accessibility Service

Physical Activity Gate uses Android's Accessibility Service to detect when a protected app comes to the foreground, including when it is shown in a floating "pop-up view" or split-screen window. To detect these floating and split-screen windows, the service reads the list of currently open windows and, for each one, only the package name of the app that owns it. It does not read on-screen text, images, or other content, does not record keystrokes, and does not capture screenshots. This information is used solely, and entirely on your device, to determine whether a physical activity gate should be shown, and is never transmitted anywhere.

5. Usage Statistics (Optional)

If you grant Usage Access permission and enable "Usage statistics" in Settings, the app reads how many minutes you spent in each protected app today. This data is read locally from Android's UsageStatsManager and is never transmitted off your device.

6. Advertising (Free Plan)

Free users see banner and interstitial ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. Pro subscribers see zero ads and are not subject to any AdMob data collection by this app. You can upgrade to Pro at any time from the app's Settings or Subscription screen to remove all ads.

7. In-App Purchases

Subscription purchases are handled entirely by Google Play Billing. Physical Activity Gate does not receive or store your payment information. Purchase verification is performed client-side using the Play Billing Library. For subscription management, visit your Google Play account.

8. Data Storage and Security

All app data is stored in an encrypted local SQLite database managed by Android's Room persistence library. Your PIN (if set) is stored as a one-way cryptographic hash — the plain-text PIN is never saved. No data is backed up to external cloud services beyond Android's standard local backup, which you can disable in your Android settings.

9. Data Sharing

Physical Activity Gate does not sell, share, rent, or disclose your personal data to any third party except as follows:

  • Google AdMob — for ad serving on the free plan only (see Section 6).
  • Google Play Billing — for subscription purchase processing only (see Section 7).
  • Legal obligations — if required by applicable law.

10. Children's Privacy

Physical Activity Gate is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through this app, please contact us using the details below.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be revised. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
